About PicStudio

What we do

PicStudio is a set of image tools that run in your browser. No software to install and no account to register — just open the page to cut out backgrounds, blur backgrounds, convert formats, resize, compress, apply filters, round corners and build icons.

The site currently offers 8 tools, each on its own page and ready to use.

Why we never upload your images

Uploading an image to a server means three things: waiting for the upload, the server being able to see your photo, and the possibility of it being retained. PicStudio does every calculation in your browser, processing pixels locally with Canvas, so image data never leaves your device.

The benefits are obvious: faster (no upload or download), more private (we never touch your image), and unaffected by network conditions.

Is it free?

Yes — every tool is free, with no usage limits and no login required.

The site is sustained by advertising. We use Google AdSense, which is why you see ad slots and a cookie notice. Ads never affect how the tools work or how often you can use them.

Limits of what we can do

We try to be honest about what each tool can and cannot do. Background removal, for instance, is based on colour-difference analysis: it suits solid backgrounds and struggles with hair, gradients and semi-transparent objects. Blur and lossy compression are irreversible.

These notes appear in the explanation area of every tool page, not hidden away. If a tool behaves differently from its description, please tell us via the Contact page.
