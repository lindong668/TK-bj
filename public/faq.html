FAQ

Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. Everything runs in your browser; images are never uploaded to any server and we keep no copies. Results disappear from memory when you close the page.
Do I need to sign up?
No. PicStudio is completely free — just open it and start. No registration or login needed.
Which image formats are supported?
Input supports PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, BMP, GIF and other common formats. Output depends on the tool: choose PNG for cut-outs and rounded corners to keep transparency; compression outputs JPG.
Is there a usage limit?
There are no limits — use every tool as often as you like, with no daily cap.
Do you add watermarks?
No. No PicStudio tool adds a watermark.
What image sizes are supported?
We recommend under 5 MB per image with edges no longer than 4000 px. Larger images take longer locally and are more likely to hit browser memory limits.
Why does background removal struggle with some photos?
Background removal here samples the colours at the four corners and analyses edges; it works best on solid backgrounds. Gradients, hair, semi-transparent objects or subjects close in colour to the background tend to leave residue or gaps — for those, use dedicated cut-out software.
Are results stored on your servers?
No. Results exist only in the memory of your current tab and vanish when you close it — download them promptly.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. The layout adapts to mobile and your phone browser can upload gallery photos directly, though large images process noticeably slower than on a computer.
There are ads — do they affect anything?
The site is funded by Google AdSense advertising. Ads do not affect any tool's features or usage limits, nor the image processing results.
