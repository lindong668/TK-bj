Start from your task, not the tool name

Most people do not search for “canvas export”; they search for “make a favicon” or “photo too big for the form”. Each entry below gives the tool order, one sentence on what to watch, and the long read if you want the reasoning.
1

Turn one transparent PNG into favicon.ico

Tool order: icon-maker → square crop → download the 6-size ICO

Watch out: It must still read at 16 px; preview small before uploading.

guide · guide · guide

2

Convert HEIC to JPG so anyone can open it

Tool order: convert → JPG → quality 85+

Watch out: HEIC fails on some Android and older Windows builds; convert first, then compress.

guide

3

Split a long screenshot into equal parts

Tool order: split → 3–4 parts → download all slices

Watch out: Avoid cutting through a line of text; check each slice.

guide

4

Lay out multiple photos as an A4 document

Tool order: trim-edges → to-pdf → A4 / sharpness 85

Watch out: Trim the desk and white margins first, then pick one page size.

guide · guide

5

Crop a photo to the exact pixels a form requires

Tool order: crop (295×413 etc.) → id-photo rotate → compress

Watch out: Crop first, compress last; DPI numbers are not pixel dimensions.

guide · guide

6

Shrink file size without visible blur

Tool order: compress → quality 65–75 → max width 1000

Watch out: Reduce dimensions before quality — it stays sharper.

guide

7

Get a transparent PNG with clean edges

Tool order: icon-maker (transparent ICO export)

Watch out: We do not auto-cut out backgrounds; bring a transparent PNG.

guide · guide

8

Generate a scannable QR code with quiet zone

Tool order: qrcode → ECC M → download PNG

Watch out: Quiet zones and contrast matter more than size; short URLs print better.

guide

9

Add arrows and step numbers to a screenshot

Tool order: image-annotate → arrow / box / number → generate PNG

Watch out: Use at least a 3 px stroke and a contrasting colour.

10

Remove GPS and device data from EXIF

Tool order: exif → inspect → strip metadata

Watch out: Platforms may keep GPS; strip it yourself for originals.

guide · guide

11

Denoise, sharpen and upscale moderately

Tool order: restore → denoise → upscale

Watch out: Repair before enlarging; one side is capped at 4000 px.

12

Extract 8 dominant colours for design

Tool order: palette → 8 colours → copy HEX

Watch out: The palette is quantised; do not treat it as a brand ink recipe.

guide
