FAQ

Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. Everything happens locally in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, so your privacy is fully protected.
Do I need to sign up?
No. PicStudio is completely free — just open it and start. No registration needed.
Which image formats are supported?
PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, BMP and GIF are supported for both input and output.
Is there a usage limit?
No limits. You can use every tool as many times as you like.
Are processed images stored on your servers?
No. Results live only in the memory of your current browser tab and disappear when you close the page. Download them promptly — we keep no copies.
Do you add a watermark?
No. Images from every PicStudio tool come without any watermark.
What image sizes are supported?
We recommend under 5 MB per image with edges no longer than 4000 px. Larger images take longer to process locally and are more likely to hit memory limits. Photos shot on a phone can usually be processed directly.
Why does background removal struggle with some photos?
Background removal here samples the colours at the four corners and analyses edges; it works best on solid backgrounds such as plain walls, backdrops and product shots on white. Gradients, hair, semi-transparent objects or subjects close in colour to the background tend to leave residue or gaps — for those, use dedicated cut-out software.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. The layout adapts to mobile, and your phone browser can upload photos straight from the gallery. Large images will process noticeably slower on a phone than on a computer.
