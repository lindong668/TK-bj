This is our only official contact channel. Whether a tool broke, a result disappointed you, you have a feature idea or a privacy question, this inbox is the place.

What to include in your message

So we can locate and fix problems quickly, please include: the tool name, your browser and version (for example Chrome 130 or Safari 17), your device type (computer or phone), and exactly what happened.

Attaching the original image helps a lot if you can. Note that email passes through your provider, so if the image is sensitive, just describe the problem — we will never insist on the file.

Response time

We check email on business days and usually reply within 3 to 7 working days. For bug reports, we will let you know once it is fixed.

Privacy requests

If you would like to access, correct or delete data related to you, mention it in your email. Note that because all processing is local we do not store your images and do not ask for personal information, so there is usually no user data to look up.
