ID Photo Sizes Explained: 1-Inch, 2-Inch, Visas, Social Security and Exams

Most rejected ID photos fail on size. This guide collects the common specifications in both millimetres and pixels, and explains why the same "1-inch" photo has different pixel dimensions — it comes down to DPI.

Millimetres to pixels: the conversion

Specifications are usually given in millimetres (such as 25×35 mm) while upload forms want pixels. DPI bridges the two:

Pixels = millimetres ÷ 25.4 × DPI

  • 300 DPI — the print standard. 25×35 mm becomes 295×413 px.
  • 350 DPI — required by some visas. 25×35 mm becomes 344×482 px.

This is why the same "1-inch" photo has different pixel counts. Always follow the pixel figure your target system states; the millimetres are only a reference.

Requirements beyond size that cause rejections

  • Head proportion — most require the head (chin to crown) to occupy 70%–80% of the image height.
  • Background colour — white, light blue and light grey are most common. It must be clean, with no shadows or objects.
  • Recency — visas usually require the photo to be taken within the last six months.
  • Expression and accessories — typically no headwear, no glasses (or non-reflective lenses), no visible teeth, both ears showing, neutral expression.
  • File size — commonly limited to somewhere between 20 KB and 500 KB, so compression is often needed too.

Making your own ID photo: the steps

  1. Shoot a usable base photo. Face-on, even lighting, plain background, level shoulders. A phone rear camera is far sharper than the front one.
  2. Confirm the target size. Find the pixel dimensions and background colour the system requires.
  3. Crop to the ratio. Use the ID Photo tool, pick the size, and position the face so the head proportion is right.
  4. Swap the background if needed. If the original backdrop is wrong, cut out the portrait and place it on a solid colour.
  5. Compress to the limit. Use Compress or Resize to bring the file under the KB cap.
  6. Preview before submitting. Check in the upload box that nothing is cropped or distorted.

FAQ about ID photo sizes

I followed the "1-inch" spec and was still rejected. Why?
"1-inch" only defines millimetres, and systems use different DPI values (300 or 350), producing different pixel counts. Make the photo to the exact pixel dimensions your target system states.
What DPI is 295×413?
It is 25×35 mm at 300 DPI. The formula is pixels = millimetres ÷ 25.4 × DPI.
Can the background be changed with a tool?
Yes. Cut out the portrait and composite it onto a solid colour. Check that hair and shoulder edges still look natural.
The photo is too big to upload. What now?
Most systems also cap file size (commonly 20 KB–500 KB). Work backwards with the Compress tool: dimensions first, then quality. Two or three passes usually get there.
Can I use a casual photo?
Not recommended. ID photos have rules about lighting, background, head proportion and expression. Cropped casual shots are usually rejected for cluttered backgrounds or odd angles — take a fresh front-facing photo instead.

证件照被退回，八成是尺寸不对。这篇文章汇总常见证件的规格（毫米 + 像素对照），并讲清为什么同样叫「一寸」，不同系统要求的像素数却不一样——关键在于 DPI。

先搞懂毫米和像素怎么换算

规格表通常给的是毫米（比如 25×35mm），但上传系统要的是像素。两者靠 DPI 换算：

像素 = 毫米 ÷ 25.4 × DPI

  • 300 DPI：打印标准。25×35mm → 295×413 像素。
  • 350 DPI：部分签证要求。25×35mm → 344×482 像素。

这就是「同样是一寸，像素却不同」的原因。务必以目标系统给出的像素数为准，表格里的毫米只是参考。

除了尺寸，这些要求同样会导致被退回

  • 头部占比：多数要求头部（下巴到头顶）占照片高度的 70%–80%，过小过大都不行。
  • Background colour：白、浅蓝、浅灰最常见，具体看系统要求。背景必须纯净无阴影、无杂物。
  • Capture time：签证通常要求近 6 个月内拍摄。
  • 表情与配饰：多数要求免冠、不戴眼镜（或镜片不反光）、不露齿、双耳可见、表情自然。
  • 文件大小：常见限制 20KB–500KB 不等，需配合压缩使用。

自己制作证件照的步骤

  1. 拍一张合格的底片。正面、光线均匀、背景纯净、肩膀平齐，用手机后置摄像头拍比前置清晰得多。
  2. 确定目标尺寸。查清系统要求的像素数和Backdrop。
  3. 裁剪到目标比例。用「证件照」工具选对应规格，调整人脸位置使头部占比合适。
  4. 换底色（如需）。若原背景不符合要求，用「去背景」工具抠出人像后替换为纯色底。
  5. 压到限制大小。用「图片压缩」或「尺寸调整」把文件压到目标 KB 以内。
  6. 提交前预览。在系统上传框里预览一次，确认没有被裁切或变形。

关于证件照尺寸的常见问题

为什么我按「一寸」做了还是被退回？
因为「一寸」只规定了毫米尺寸，而不同系统采用的 DPI 不同（300 或 350），换算出的像素数就不一样。请直接按目标系统给出的像素要求制作。
295×413 是多少 DPI？
对应 25×35mm 在 300 DPI 下的像素数。换算公式：像素 = 毫米 ÷ 25.4 × DPI。
底色能用工具改吗？
可以。用「去背景」工具把人像抠出，再合成到纯色背景上。注意抠图后检查发丝、肩膀边缘是否自然。
照片太大传不上去怎么办？
多数系统还限制文件大小（常见 20KB–500KB）。用「图片压缩」按目标大小反推：先缩尺寸再调质量，通常两三次就能压进去。
可以用生活照改吗？
不建议。证件照对光线、背景、头部占比、表情都有要求。用生活照裁剪出来的往往因背景杂乱或角度问题被退回，最好重新拍一张正面照。
