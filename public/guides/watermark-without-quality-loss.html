How to Add a Watermark Without Ruining Your Image Quality

A watermark is supposed to protect your work, not wreck it. Most bad watermarks fail for the same few reasons: they sit where a crop removes them, they are so opaque they fight the photo, or they are applied at the wrong point in the workflow and get compressed twice. This guide covers all three.

Visible and invisible watermarking are different jobs

  • Visible watermark — text or a logo drawn onto the pixels. It deters casual reuse and advertises ownership. Anyone with an editor can attempt to remove it.
  • Invisible watermark — data embedded in the pixels or written into the file's metadata. It cannot be seen, but it can prove ownership later. It survives cropping less well and is stripped by many social platforms.

They solve different problems. A visible mark discourages; an invisible one evidences. For most people sharing work online, the visible mark is what matters, and it is what the rest of this guide covers.

Where to place it: think about cropping

The classic bottom-right corner is the first thing a crop removes. Social platforms also crop aggressively — the same image is shown square in a feed, tall in a story, and wide in a preview. A corner mark can be outside the visible area in all three.

PlacementSurvives croppingDistracts from subject
Single cornerPoorLow
Centred, low opacityGoodMedium — sits over the subject
Tiled / repeated across the frameBestHigh, but hardest to remove
Edge-anchored bandMediumLow

If the image is valuable, tile it. A repeated pattern cannot be cropped out and cannot be cloned away in one pass. If the image is mainly for viewing, a single centred mark at low opacity is the better compromise.

How transparent should it be?

Somewhere between 15% and 30% opacity for most photographs. The goal is a mark that is clearly readable when someone looks for it, but does not pull attention in normal viewing.

  • Too faint (under 10%) — disappears on bright areas and is trivially removed by brightness or contrast adjustment.
  • Too strong (over 50%) — becomes part of the composition and genuinely damages the image.
  • On dark photos use a light mark; on light photos use a dark one. A single fixed colour will vanish on part of the frame.

A thin outline or soft drop shadow does more for legibility on busy backgrounds than increasing opacity does. Use those first.

The order of operations matters more than you think

This is the mistake that actually costs quality. Every time you re-save a JPEG, the compression runs again on the whole image — including the watermark's edges. Do this and the mark develops ragged artefacts:

  • resize → crop → colour adjust → compress → watermark

should instead be:

  • resize → crop → colour adjust → watermark → export once

In short: watermark last, export once. If you must deliver at a smaller size, go back to the un-watermarked master, resize, then watermark again — never re-compress an already watermarked file.

Why the export format matters

JPEG compression works in 8×8 pixel blocks and discards fine detail first. Sharp edges are exactly the fine detail it discards, so a crisp watermark on a JPEG picks up ringing — faint halos along every stroke — and the effect gets worse each time the file is saved.

  • PNG — lossless, keeps edges clean. The right choice for screenshots, graphics and anything with text in it. Larger file.
  • JPEG at high quality (85–95) — acceptable for photographs. Fine for a single export; poor if the file will be re-saved.
  • WebP — smaller than JPEG at similar quality and handles edges better. Good for web delivery if your audience's browsers support it.

What a watermark does and does not do

It does not prevent copying, and it does not create legal ownership — in most jurisdictions copyright exists the moment you make the work. What it does is establish that the image was published by you with a claim attached, which makes casual misuse less likely and deliberate misuse harder to excuse.

For anything you genuinely need to protect, pair the visible mark with the practical measures: publish at a resolution that is not worth stealing, keep the full-size master private, and register the work where registration is available and useful to you.

Frequently asked questions

What opacity should a watermark be?
Around 15–30% for photographs. Below 10% it disappears on bright areas; above 50% it starts to damage the picture. Add a subtle outline or shadow rather than increasing opacity.
Should I put the watermark in the corner?
A single corner is the easiest placement to crop away, and social platforms crop aggressively. For anything valuable, tile the mark across the frame instead.
Should I watermark before or after resizing?
After. Resizing resamples the whole image including the watermark, which softens and weakens it. Watermark as the final step, then export once.
Why does my watermark look fuzzy after saving as JPEG?
JPEG discards fine detail in 8×8 blocks, and sharp edges are the first thing lost — producing faint halos called ringing. Export to PNG if the image contains text, or use JPEG quality 85+ and avoid re-saving.
Can I add the same watermark to a whole batch of photos?
Yes, and you should keep it consistent: same file, same relative size and position. Scale the mark proportionally to each image rather than using one fixed pixel size, or it will look huge on small photos and tiny on large ones.

加水印不伤画质：位置、透明度和操作顺序

水印本该保护作品，而不是毁掉它。糟糕的水印失败原因就那么几个：放在一裁就没的地方、不透明度太高跟照片打架、或者在错误的步骤添加导致被压缩两次。这篇逐个讲清楚。

可见水印和不可见水印是两回事

  • 可见水印：文字或 logo 直接画在像素上。作用是劝退随手盗用、宣示归属。会编辑的人都能试着把它抹掉。
  • 不可见水印：把数据嵌进像素或写进文件元数据。看不见，但日后能用来证明归属。抗裁剪能力差，而且很多社交平台会自动剥离元数据。

两者解决的问题不同：可见的是劝阻，不可见的是取证。对多数在网上分享作品的人来说，重要的是可见水印，下面也主要讲这个。

放在哪：先想清楚「会被裁掉吗」

经典的右下角位置，恰恰是裁剪时第一个被切掉的。社交平台的裁剪还很激进——同一张图在信息流里是方的、在故事里是竖的、在预览里是宽的，角上的水印可能三种情况下都看不到。

放置方式抗裁剪对主体的干扰
单个角落差低
居中、低不透明度好中——会压在主体上
满屏平铺重复最好高，但最难去除
贴边条带中低

如果图确实值钱，用平铺。重复的图案裁不掉，也没法一次性修掉。如果图主要是给人看的，单个居中、低不透明度是更合适的折中。

不透明度多少合适？

多数照片在 15% 到 30% 之间。目标是：特意找的时候一眼能看清，正常观看时不抢注意力。

  • 太淡（低于 10%）：亮部区域直接消失，调一下亮度对比度就抹掉了。
  • 太重（高于 50%）：变成画面的一部分，真的会伤图。
  • 深色照片用浅色水印，浅色照片用深色水印。固定一个颜色总会在画面某个区域消失。

在杂乱背景上，加一道细描边或柔和阴影，比单纯提高不透明度更能提升可读性。优先用这两个。

操作顺序比你想的更重要

这才是真正会损失画质的错误。每次重新保存 JPEG，压缩都会对整张图再跑一遍——包括水印的边缘。顺序错了，水印边缘就会出现毛刺：

  • 缩放 → 裁剪 → 调色 → 压缩 → 加水印　（错）

正确顺序是：

  • 缩放 → 裁剪 → 调色 → 加水印 → 只导出一次

一句话：水印放最后，只导出一次。如果必须再出一个小尺寸版本，回到没加水印的原稿，重新缩放后再加水印——绝不要把已经加水印的文件再压一遍。

为什么导出格式很关键

JPEG 压缩以 8×8 像素块为单位工作，并且优先丢弃精细细节。而锐利的边缘正是它最先丢弃的东西——所以 JPEG 上清晰的水印会出现振铃（每一笔周围淡淡的晕圈），而且每保存一次就更明显。

  • PNG：无损，边缘干净。截图、图形、任何含文字的图都应该用它。缺点是文件大。
  • 高质量 JPEG（85–95）：照片可以接受。单次导出没问题，反复保存就糟糕了。
  • WebP：同等画质下比 JPEG 小，边缘处理也更好。网页交付的好选择，前提是受众浏览器支持。

水印能做什么、不能做什么

它不能阻止别人复制，也不能创造法律上的权属——在多数司法辖区，作品完成的那一刻版权就存在了。它的作用是：证明这张图是你带着权属声明发布的，从而降低随手滥用的概率，也让恶意使用更难辩解。

真正需要保护的图，请把可见水印和这些实际手段搭配使用：发布一个「不值得偷」的分辨率、把全尺寸原稿留在自己手里、以及在有登记制度且对你有用的地方做作品登记。

常见问题

水印的不透明度设多少？
照片大约 15–30%。低于 10% 在亮部会消失，高于 50% 就开始伤图。加细描边或阴影比提高不透明度更划算。
水印应该放在角落吗？
单个角落最容易一裁就没，而社交平台裁剪很激进。值钱的图建议改成满屏平铺。
应该在缩放之前还是之后加水印？
之后。缩放会重采样整张图包括水印，会让它变虚变弱。水印作为最后一步，然后只导出一次。
为什么存成 JPEG 后水印变模糊了？
JPEG 按 8×8 块丢弃细节，锐利边缘首当其冲，产生叫「振铃」的淡晕。含文字的图导出 PNG，或者用 85 以上质量的 JPEG 并避免反复保存。
能给一批照片批量加同一个水印吗？
可以，而且要保持一致：同一个文件、相同的相对尺寸和位置。水印要按每张图等比缩放，而不是固定一个像素尺寸——否则小图上显得巨大，大图上又太小。
