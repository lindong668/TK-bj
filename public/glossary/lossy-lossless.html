Lossy and lossless

What JPEG throws away, what PNG keeps, and why re-saving hurts.

What JPEG throws away, what PNG keeps, and why re-saving hurts.

Lossy compression discards detail the eye is less likely to notice; lossless compression only reorganises data so the original can be rebuilt exactly. JPEG is lossy, PNG is lossless, WebP supports both. Every lossy re-save loses a little more — generational loss.

You see it when a screenshot saved as JPEG has soft text, a logo shows blocky edges, or a file edited ten times keeps getting worse. Use PNG or high-quality WebP when text and hard edges matter.

Our compressor re-encodes with a quality setting and optional width limit; Convert Format offers JPG, PNG and WebP. For text, prefer PNG; for photos, JPEG at 70–85 is usually enough.

Common misconception: saving a JPEG as PNG restores quality. Lost detail does not come back; it merely stops getting worse.
