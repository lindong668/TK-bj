ICC profiles

The tag that says what your RGB numbers actually mean.

The tag that says what your RGB numbers actually mean.

sRGB, Display P3 and Adobe RGB are different gamuts. An ICC profile tells software how to map numeric values to real colours. Without a profile, browsers usually assume sRGB. A P3 file opened by software that ignores the tag can look dull or oversaturated.

You meet this when a design looks vivid on a Mac and grey on Windows, when a camera exports Adobe RGB straight to the web, or when a printer asks for CMYK.

Our tools work in sRGB on canvas and export PNG/JPEG without a custom profile — a safe default for screens. For print, convert to CMYK and embed the target profile in a desktop editor.

Common misconception: adding an sRGB tag fixes wrong colours. A tag only explains the numbers; it does not change them.
