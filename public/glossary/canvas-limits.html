Browser canvas limits

Why very large images fail silently, and the caps this site applies.

Why very large images fail silently, and the caps this site applies.

Canvas pixels live in memory at four bytes each: 4000×4000 is 64 MB, 8000×8000 is 256 MB. Browsers also cap the maximum side and total area. Chrome usually allows up to 16384 px per side; Safari and mobile are lower and vary by device.

You hit the limit when stitching a very long screenshot, enlarging a scan, or rendering many PDF pages at once. Symptoms include a black preview, a zero-byte export, or a frozen tab.

Our editing tools cap the working side at 3000 px and crop/upscale at 4000 px; larger requests are scaled down first. Split long images into parts and stitch afterwards.

Common misconception: a machine with more RAM cannot fail. Browsers also have per-canvas area and GPU texture limits.
