Canvas export formats

PNG, JPEG and WebP are native; ICO, PDF and SVG are not.

PNG, JPEG and WebP are native; ICO, PDF and SVG are not.

canvas.toDataURL() and toBlob() support image/png, image/jpeg and image/webp. JPEG has no alpha, so transparent areas get a fill. WebP may fall back to PNG on old Safari. ICO, PDF and SVG are not canvas formats.

You notice problems when an export grows unexpectedly, transparency turns black, the extension does not match the real encoding, or a data URL pasted into CSS bloats the page.

Our editing tools export PNG by default; Convert Format offers JPG/WebP/ICO; Images to PDF re-wraps pages with a PDF library; the Base64 tool produces a PNG data URL. Extensions always match the encoding.

Common misconception: renaming .png to .jpg converts it. The bytes are unchanged and upload forms still reject it.
