Vector and raster

Pixels versus paths, and when enlarging is hopeless.

Pixels versus paths, and when enlarging is hopeless.

Raster images (PNG, JPEG, WebP) are grids of pixels; enlarging beyond the original resolution softens them. Vector images (SVG, AI, EPS) are paths and fill rules and scale without loss. Browsers display SVG, but favicons and some system icons still require bitmaps.

You meet it when a small logo is blown up for a poster, a screenshot is treated as vector art, or an “SVG” turns out to contain an embedded bitmap.

This site works on raster images, with pixel caps on resize, crop and upscale. The icon tool writes a multi-size ICO; make the SVG in a vector editor and use this site for the bitmap fallbacks.

Common misconception: a .svg extension guarantees vector content. It may be a wrapped PNG.
