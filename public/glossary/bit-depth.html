8-bit versus 16-bit colour, and where banding comes from.

Bit depth is how many bits each channel uses. 8 bits per channel gives 256 levels and about 16.7 million colours; 16 bits gives far finer steps and is used for RAW, scanning and print retouching. Browser canvas is effectively 8-bit, so anything wider is quantised.

You meet it when a sky gradient shows visible bands, a black-and-white conversion breaks into steps, or a 16-bit TIFF shrinks dramatically after passing through a web tool.

Our tools process and export 8-bit PNG/JPEG — enough for screens and social. Keep the original 16-bit file if you plan more print retouching.

Common misconception: 8-bit is always insufficient. For screen output it is usually fine; repeated lossy compression causes more damage.
