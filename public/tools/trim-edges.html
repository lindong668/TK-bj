How to use this tool

  1. Upload a screenshot, scan or product shot with a uniform border.
  2. Keep "four corners" sampling, or pick the exact edge colour yourself.
  3. Raise tolerance if the border has slight compression noise or a soft gradient; lower it for strict matching.
  4. Trim and download. The result info shows exactly how many pixels were removed on each side.

Note: All processing happens in your browser: images are never uploaded. Gradients and shadows are the known limit of this automatic method.

How it works

The tool reads the image into a pixel buffer, samples the border colour from the four corners, then walks inward: a row is considered empty when all sampled pixels match that colour within the tolerance, and likewise for columns. The bounding box of non-empty pixels is kept.

It is a deterministic formula, not a learned model. That means gradients, drop shadows and anti-aliased edges can be misread as content — the tolerance slider and the result readout let you check before downloading.

Frequently asked questions

Why was nothing trimmed?
The border is probably a gradient, has a shadow, or differs from the four corners. Raise tolerance or pick the edge colour manually.
Why did it crop too much?
Tolerance is too high and real content near the edge matches the background. Lower it and try again.
Does it work on transparent images?
Yes. Fully transparent pixels count as background, so transparent padding is trimmed too.
Is my image uploaded?
No. The pixel scan runs locally in your browser.
