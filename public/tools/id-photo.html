How to use this tool

  1. Upload your image: photos taken on a phone, scans and screenshots all work.
  2. Use quick buttons for 90° / 180° turns or a horizontal / vertical flip; stack them if needed.
  3. Fine-tune with the free-angle slider (-180° to 180°) and choose what fills the new corners.
  4. Apply and download the PNG. Transparent corners stay transparent.

Note: All processing happens in your browser: images are never uploaded. Download the result before closing the page.

How it works

A 90° turn simply moves pixels to new coordinates, so it is lossless. A free-angle rotation re-samples the image: the canvas is enlarged to the rotated bounding box, the image is drawn through a rotation transform, and the new corners are filled with the colour you picked.

Flipping is a negative scale on one axis. Both operations run on an off-screen canvas with high-quality smoothing, and the result is exported as PNG so transparency is preserved.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 90° rotation lossless?
Yes for 90° and 180°: no interpolation is involved, pixels are just relocated. Any other angle resamples the image.
Why are there empty triangles after rotating?
A rotated rectangle no longer fills its axis-aligned bounding box. Choose a fill colour, or keep it transparent and export PNG.
Can I rotate a JPG and keep transparency?
JPG has no alpha channel. If you need transparent corners, download as PNG (the default here).
Are my photos uploaded?
No. Rotation and flipping are Canvas operations inside your browser; the file never leaves your device.
