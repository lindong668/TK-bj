How to crop

  1. Upload an image
  2. Pick a target platform in the size presets (e.g. Xiaohongshu 1080×1440) and the dimensions fill in automatically
  3. Choose a crop mode: "Fill" to cover the frame, "Fit" if you worry about cutting off faces
  4. Tap "Crop image" to preview, then download once you are happy

How it works

Cropping works in two steps: take the largest centred rectangle matching the target ratio, then scale it to the target size. Because it is the "largest rectangle", the frame is always filled with no empty edges.

"Fit" mode takes the other route: scale the whole image down until it fits inside the target box, then fill the leftover space with your chosen colour. You get borders, but not a single pixel is lost.

Scaling uses the browser Canvas high-quality interpolation (imageSmoothingQuality=high), which looks far better than plain stretching and keeps edges from going soft when enlarging.

FAQ about cropping

Why did my image get blurry after cropping?

If the original is smaller than the target, you are effectively enlarging, and every tool loses sharpness doing that. Use a source that is large enough, or run it through the Upscale tool first.

Will "Fill" cut people out?

It can. Fill takes the largest centred rectangle, so a subject off to one side may get clipped. Switch to "Fit", or recompose first.

Are the ID photo sizes standard?

1-inch 295×413 and 2-inch 413×579 are common pixel specs (25×35 mm and 35×49 mm at 300 dpi). Always follow what your issuing authority asks for — some cases differ slightly.

Can I crop several images at once?

This version handles one image at a time. For batches, merge them with the Merge tool first, or process them one by one.
