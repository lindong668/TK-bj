How to use this tool

  1. Upload image A in the main upload area.
  2. Upload image B in the second box.
  3. Drag the slider to reveal A on the left and B on the right; tick "highlight differences" for a pixel diff map.
  4. Download the comparison canvas as a PNG.

Note: All processing happens in your browser: images are never uploaded. Download the comparison before closing the page.

How it works

Both images are drawn to off-screen canvases on a shared coordinate system. The slider simply clips the draw calls at a chosen x position; the difference mode subtracts RGB values pixel by pixel and marks any channel sum above the threshold.

When the two images have different dimensions, B can be stretched to A's canvas so the comparison stays aligned. Everything is computed in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

What if the two images are different sizes?
Keep "stretch B" ticked to align them to A's canvas, or untick it to draw B at native size from the top-left.
Why does the difference map show noise?
JPEG compression and slight re-encoding shift pixel values. Lower your expectations for lossy sources; PNG gives a clean diff.
Can I compare a HEIC photo?
Yes. HEIC/HEIF is decoded locally in a Web Worker when the browser cannot read it natively.
Are images uploaded?
No. Both images stay in your browser and are drawn to local canvases.
