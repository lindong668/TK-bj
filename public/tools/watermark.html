How to add a watermark

  1. Upload the image you want watermarked (drag and drop works)
  2. Choose "Text" or "Image" watermark; for an image watermark, upload your logo first
  3. Set the position: "Tile across image" for anti-theft, "Bottom right" for a signature
  4. Adjust opacity and size, click "Add watermark" to preview, then download

How it works

A watermark is simply a semi-transparent layer composited over the image. The tool copies the original onto a canvas in memory, draws your text or logo at the opacity you set, and blends the two pixel layers proportionally.

"Tile" first rotates the canvas (by −30° by default), then repeats the mark row by row across an enlarged area. Coverage is maximal: crop off any corner and watermarks remain elsewhere, making it the strongest anti-theft option.

Compositing uses the Canvas globalAlpha blend and never damages original pixels — because it works on a copy, you can retune and redo at any time with no cumulative quality loss.

FAQ about watermarking

Does watermarking reduce quality?

No. The watermark is a layer over a copy; original pixels are never recompressed. Export as PNG and quality matches the original.

Can a tiled watermark be cropped out?

Barely. Tiling repeats at −30° across the whole image, so cropping any corner leaves marks elsewhere. A single bottom-right mark is easy to crop, so tiling is strongly recommended for protection.

Why use a logo with a transparent background?

An opaque logo brings a white or black block that looks bad on top of an image. A transparent PNG shows only the artwork and blends naturally. If your logo is a JPG, remove its background with the Background Remover first.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs through Canvas in your browser and the image never leaves your device — which also means speed depends on your computer.
