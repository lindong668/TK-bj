How to extract colours

  1. Upload the image you want colours from
  2. Drag the slider to choose how many colours (3–16)
  3. Click "Extract" and a swatch card appears below
  4. Click any value to copy it, or "Copy all" to take the whole set

How it works

Extraction uses K-means clustering. Treat every pixel colour as a point in 3D space, place K random centres, then repeatedly assign each point to its nearest centre and recompute the centres until they settle.

The K settled centres are the image's K dominant colours. For stability the algorithm samples (large images are not computed pixel by pixel) and iterates 12 times.

Finally the colours are sorted by luma (0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B) from dark to light, ready to use as a palette strip.

FAQ about colour extraction

The colours look off — what can I do?

K-means starts from random centres, so repeated runs on the same image can differ slightly. Try a different count, or run it a few times and keep the colours that appear consistently.

Why are very bright or dark colours missing?

Clustering is driven by pixel counts, so colours with a tiny share (a small highlight, say) may not form their own cluster. Increase the number of colours in that case.

Do transparent areas affect the result?

No. Pixels below 50% opacity are skipped, so only visible parts are sampled.

Can I save the swatch card as an image?

This version only copies values. For an image, take a screenshot or merge the swatches with the Merge tool.
